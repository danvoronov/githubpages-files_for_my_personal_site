Interdisciplinary Art Projects


Contemporary ritual room

Summer 2019. I created a symbol system and painted "ritual" graffiti on it. This is an unfinished building of a hospital for the mentally ill. Last floor.


Interactive online drama: "In the homeless world"

2026. Online interactive play: https://apps.danvoronov.com/unhoused/. An interactive documentary drama where four characters meet around a campfire in the night forest and share their stories about life without a home.


Spring 2019. Telegram bots reading of our drama project with Julia Gonchar (see theater page). I made 4 bots that became "actors" "reading" our play every day in a public chat, in real time. When they finished, all the text "burned" out.

You can find details in the article (Russian).


Web art and installation "Chuttya"

Summer 2018. I took part in "Summer architecture school at Soshenko 33" and created a media capture of the "spirit" of this house.

One of my topics was #between, so I found it interesting to draw attention to doors. Closed doors.

The web address of the project is http://danvoronov.com/prj/soshenko33/


You can read details in the article (Russian).

Makes me happy

Interactive theater project aimed at finding the best and most positive manifestations of life around us.

2016 spring to autumn. I asked everyone through Facebook, including my friends to send me texts about what makes them happy. I also asked them to add titles of their favorite songs.

We used 9 stories by Oleksandra Kosmі, Svit in, Ott, Julia Gonchar, D, z-nova, Alice Kino, Dan Voronov, Julia Solnechna.
In addition to these texts I added excerpts from books on positive psychology.

We made three performances with the support of PostPlayTheater (Kyiv) and actors (Galina Dzhikaeva, Oleg Shurigin) and independent actors (Olya Proskura, Natalia Zagasheva).


Facebook public "Makes me happy" facebook.com/menya.raduet.project with posts on positive-position themes was online part of the project.

Video record is available on YouTube.
